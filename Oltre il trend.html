OLTRE IL TREND

/ EDITORIAL DESIGN
/ MASTER THESIS PROJECT
/ 2026

The aim of this thesis is to critically analyze the dynamics of virality in the beauty industry, and to highlight the advantages and risks of a trend-driven Content Strategy. In fact, beauty is one of the areas that experiments most with digital communication through short-form content on social platforms, where trends represent increasingly recurring narrative models.
The research investigates how it is possible to design a Content Strategy in the beauty industry that, while leveraging trends, remains consistent and aligned with the brand’s identity and values.
