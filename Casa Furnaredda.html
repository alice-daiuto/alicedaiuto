CASA FURNAREDDA

/ LOGO DESIGN
/ FREELANCE PROJECT
/ 2023

Logo Design for Casa Furnaredda, a B&B immersed in the natural landscape of Basilicata, a place where guests can feel at home, surrounded by a warm, familiar and relaxing atmosphere.
The logo translates this identity into a meaningful symbol. At its core is the F of Furnaredda, whose forms combine several visual references that tell the story of the place. The shape of the flame evokes the fire of the B&B’s oven, a defining element and the inspiration behind the name Furnaredda. Fire becomes a symbol of warmth, hospitality, intimacy and the feeling of being welcomed into a home. The spark symbolises the well-being and relax guests can experience during their stay.
The textured finish of both the pictogram and typography reflects the rustic and authentic soul of the place, creating a visual identity that feels warm and natural.
